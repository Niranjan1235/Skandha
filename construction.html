How We Build

Four clear stages, so you always know where your home stands.

  1. 1

    Plan and design

    Site study, floor plans, structural drawings and a clear budget.

  2. 2

    Approvals and foundation

    Permissions, soil checks, excavation and footings.

  3. 3

    Structure and shell

    Columns, slabs, masonry, plumbing and electrical rough-in.

  4. 4

    Finish and handover

    Plaster, flooring, paint, fixtures and a final quality check.
